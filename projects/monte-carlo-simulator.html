Overview

  • Two phases

    Years of saving with growing contributions, then years of inflation-adjusted withdrawals net of CPP, OAS or pension income.

  • Planner-ready outputs

    Probability of success, median values, ruin-year statistics, a percentile fan chart and checkpoint tables, in nominal or today's (real) dollars.

  • Private by design

    No market data or network access is needed. Every calculation happens on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

The model

Annual return
Drawn independently each year from a normal distribution with your expected return and volatility.
Saving years
value × (1 + return) + contribution. Contributions grow by the contribution growth rate each year. A negative balance is floored at $0 but can recover.
Retirement years
value × (1 + return) − withdrawal. The first-year withdrawal is spending minus pension income, and it grows with inflation every year.
Success
A path fails (ruin) if its value reaches $0 during retirement; it stays at $0 afterwards. Probability of success = paths that never fail ÷ all paths.
Percentiles
10th, 25th, 50th, 75th and 90th percentiles of all paths for each year, using linear interpolation.
Real dollars
Nominal value ÷ (1 + inflation)year, i.e. today's purchasing power.
Initial withdrawal rate
First-year net withdrawal ÷ median portfolio at retirement.

Troubleshooting

“CPP/OAS/Pension income must be less than annual retirement spending”

If guaranteed income already covers spending, the portfolio never needs to fund withdrawals, so there is nothing to simulate. Lower the pension figure or raise spending.

Results change slightly every run

Each run draws new random paths. Increase the number of simulations (up to 10,000) for more stable percentages.

The chart is missing

The chart uses Chart.js from a CDN. If that is blocked, the summary tiles, table and CSV export still work.

The desktop launcher stops with an error

The launcher names the component that failed and keeps a log in files/test-logs/setup-and-run-latest.log. See the setup guide.
