Overview

  • Performance & risk

    Total and annualized return, annualized volatility, Sharpe ratio, and maximum drawdown for 2–10 securities over nine horizons (1D to 5Y).

  • Correlation & regression

    A correlation heatmap computed over identical trading intervals, with shared-observation counts, diversification flags, and alpha/beta/R² regression against any benchmark.

  • Currency normalization

    Convert every series to USD, CAD, EUR, or GBP before any metric is calculated, using official Bank of Canada daily exchange rates (ECB as fallback).

  • Exports

    A five-sheet XLSX workbook plus CSV and JSON downloads, generated in your browser. The desktop app adds Excel/CSV/JPG exports and rolling charts.

Web app

Everything runs in this page and nothing is uploaded. Choose where prices come from: market data from your list of sources (Yahoo Finance with no key, and Alpha Vantage if you add your own key, tried in the order you choose in Settings), your CSV files (no account needed), or the offline demo with synthetic prices.

      Appearance

      Yahoo Finance — Free Market Data

      Prices are retrieved automatically from Yahoo Finance through this site's data gateway and briefly cached for faster access. No account or key is needed. Quotes may be delayed, incomplete, or differ from official exchange data. This information is for research and planning, not financial advice.

      For more sources, add your own provider API key and choose the order in which sources are tried.

      Yahoo Finance: checking…

      Your sources, in the order they are tried

      For each ticker, the first source that can give its price history is used; the next is tried only if it cannot (it does not cover the exchange, has no data, cannot confirm the listing or currency, or is limited). This list is shared with the Portfolio Rebalancer on this site.

      Alpha Vantage with your own key (optional)

      An optional extra source: your own free Alpha Vantage API key. Saving it switches Alpha Vantage on below Yahoo Finance in your list; move it up to try it first. It covers US and TSX/TSX-V stocks and ETFs and some US mutual funds, but not market indexes; the free plan allows 25 requests per day, and its terms allow personal, non-commercial use. Fetched prices stay in this browser, and this page never sends your key or data anywhere except Alpha Vantage.

      • Where the key is kept: by default for this tab only (session storage; the browser forgets it when the tab closes). Tick Remember on this device to keep it in local storage until you press Remove key.
      • Where it is sent: only to www.alphavantage.co, inside each request's address, which is how Alpha Vantage accepts keys.
      • Who can read it: any script running on this website can read browser storage. This page's only outside script is the chart library, loaded from jsDelivr with an integrity check so a modified copy is refused. Use a free key that you can replace.
      • Removing it: Remove key deletes the key and every response fetched with it. Changing the key does the same for the old key.

      Where the data comes from

      • Yahoo Finance: through this site's data gateway, no key (see above). One request per ticker, sent one at a time; copies saved in this browser (12 hours for history) or by the gateway are reused and labelled with their age. If Yahoo Finance asks for fewer requests, nothing more is sent to it for a while and the next source in your list is used.
      • Alpha Vantage: with your key. Requests run one at a time, at least 1.2 seconds apart, across every open tab of this site. A new ticker costs two or three requests (a listing search to confirm its currency, occasionally a second search, then its prices); data saved in this browser costs none. Alpha Vantage's free plan allows 25 requests per day.
      • Marketstack and Finnhub keys (added in the Portfolio Rebalancer) give latest prices only on this website, so they are never used for comparisons or snapshots here.
      • CSV: files you open are read in this browser and never uploaded.
      • Exchange rates for normalization: Bank of Canada daily averages; if those cannot be reached, the European Central Bank's reference rates. Both are official and need no key.
      • Demo: synthetic prices generated from a fixed seed; not market data.

      Cache

      Responses fetched with your key are saved in this browser (quotes for 15 minutes, price history for 12 hours, listing details for 30 days) to save your daily requests. They are kept for that key only: changing or removing the key deletes them, so data is never relabelled as coming from another key. A saved copy is used and labelled when a refresh fails.

      Data sources & privacy

      • Your CSV files are read in your browser and never uploaded. They work with no account and no network.
      • Yahoo Finance (no key): retrieved by this site's own data gateway, which has a fixed list of exchanges, checks every answer and caches briefly (history up to 12 hours, labelled with its age). Only ticker symbols and a period are sent, and every series shows where it came from and when. Prices may be delayed and are not official exchange data. Requests are sent one at a time; after any request to slow down, nothing more is sent to Yahoo Finance for a while.
      • Alpha Vantage with your own key (optional). With your own key, the data is licensed to you for personal use and stays in your browser. Coverage: US and TSX/TSX-V stocks and ETFs and some US mutual funds. Not covered: market indexes such as ^GSPC or ^GSPTSE (a premium feature), Canadian mutual-fund codes such as TDB902, and most other exchanges. Windows up to about four months use daily closes as traded (not adjusted for splits or dividends; unusually large moves are flagged); longer windows use weekly closes adjusted for splits and dividends.
      • Exchange rates come from the Bank of Canada's daily averages (terms), with the European Central Bank's reference rates as a fallback. Pairs not involving CAD are derived through CAD. Prices are converted on each date with the latest rate published on or before that date.
      • No public relays. Earlier versions fetched Yahoo Finance through free public CORS relays, which proved unreliable and have been removed. The only server between this page and a price source is this site's own data gateway, which never receives your keys.
      • Saved in your browser: your source order, your key (for the current tab, or until you remove it if you choose Remember on this device), responses fetched with it (kept under that key only and deleted when the key changes or is removed; see Settings for how long), a count of today's requests per key, and the theme. Nothing is stored on a server.
      • The offline demo makes no requests. Its prices are generated from a fixed seed and are clearly labelled synthetic.

      Troubleshooting

      Alpha Vantage has reached its limit

      The free Alpha Vantage plan allows 25 requests per day: each new ticker costs about two, and results are saved in this browser for reuse. When the limit is reached, Alpha Vantage is skipped until 00:00 UTC and the next source in your list is used; you can also switch to Your CSV files or the Offline demo at any time.

      Yahoo Finance is unavailable or paused

      Its entry in Settings → Your sources says why. Paused means Yahoo Finance or this site's gateway asked for fewer requests: nothing more is sent to it until the pause ends, and the next source in your list is used. Unavailable means the gateway could not be reached; Check again asks once more. Your Alpha Vantage key, your CSV files and the offline demo keep working.

      A ticker says “no data”

      Use exchange suffixes for Canadian listings (XIU.TO, .V for TSX-V). Market indexes are not available from these sources; use an index ETF or import index prices from a CSV file. Each failed ticker lists every source that was tried or skipped, and why. The rest of the comparison continues without the failed ticker.

      Charts are missing

      Charts use Chart.js from a CDN. If it is blocked, the tables, heatmap, and exports still work.

      The desktop launcher stops with an error

      The launcher names the component that failed and keeps a log in files/test-logs/setup-and-run-latest.log. See the setup guide's troubleshooting section.
