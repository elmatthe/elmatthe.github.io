Using the rebalancer

  1. Choose the settings: reporting currency, mode (New Money gives buy-only trades that never spend more than your budget; Rebalance adds no cash, so sells fund the buys), budget, and fractional or whole shares.
  2. Check your sources with Sources…: Yahoo Finance needs no key and is first by default. Optionally add your own key under API keys… (Alpha Vantage, Marketstack, Finnhub or Alpaca); it joins the list below Yahoo Finance. Reorder with Move up / Move down, Alt+Up / Alt+Down or by dragging, and switch sources on or off. Keys are sent only to their own provider (never to the site's data gateway) and never appear in exports.
  3. Enter each holding: ticker exactly as your broker shows it, shares (fractions allowed), target weight % and, optionally, the account type. Weights are relative; they're normalised to 100%. When you press Enter or Tab the ticker is identified (exchange, currency, pence or pounds) without any request and its price is fetched from the first source in your list that covers it.
  4. Press Get prices to price every holding that has no usable price yet. Canadian, US and international holdings follow the same rules; the line above the list estimates the requests per source first, names listings outside Canada and the US, and names any holding no source covers. If a source cannot give a usable price, the next one in your list is tried; each holding says which source priced it and why earlier ones failed. When it finishes, the status names each holding still without a price and why.
  5. Press Calculate plan and review the summary, the per-holding plan and any warnings. Export CSV or Excel when it is ready.

The sample portfolio

Load sample fills fourteen ordinary holdings, with no prices and targets totalling exactly 100%: VEQT.TO 305 (63%), CNQ.TO 48 (9%), XQQ.TO 27 (6.3%), HEWB.TO 14 (4.5%), AAPL 2 (1.8%), VFV.TO 8 (0.9%), SPCX 1 (0.9%), CBRS 4 (0.9%), YNVD.NE 4 (1.8%), VST 1 (0.9%), and one share each of Toyota (7203.T, JPY), SAP (SAP.DE, EUR), Vodafone (VOD.L, quoted in pence and converted to pounds) and Kweichow Moutai (600519.SS, CNY) at 2.5% each. Reporting currency CAD, New Money, budget 3,000. All fourteen are ordinary holdings, priced and planned by the same rules.

No plan is shown until every holding with shares or a target has a verified price in a confirmed currency, or a price you supplied (labelled as user-supplied everywhere). Nothing is silently guessed. Always confirm prices at your broker before trading.

Outputs

  • On screen: totals, the buy/sell/hold plan per row (shares and amount in both local and reporting currency), and warnings.
  • Excel: a new workbook with Trade Plan and Summary sheets: provider, symbol, price type, trading date and currency evidence for every holding, the exchange rates with their sources, warnings and a disclaimer.
  • CSV: the same content as one file.

Troubleshooting

A ticker isn't found, or is found in another currency

Use Yahoo-style suffixes: .TO (TSX), .V (TSX-V), .NE (Cboe Canada), .L (London, priced in pence and converted to pounds), .DE (Xetra). The currency is identified for you. YNVD.NE, and YNVD.TO as Questrade shows the same fund, are recognised as the Cboe Canada ETF; Yahoo Finance covers it (asked as YNVD.NE on Cboe Canada, in CAD); the free plans of the key providers do not. Yahoo Finance also covers Tokyo listings such as 7203.T; among the key providers only Marketstack does.

Live prices didn't load

Open API keys…: it shows how many requests this computer sent and, if the provider reported a daily or monthly limit, its own wording and when it resets. A "slow down" notice is retried automatically. No plan is shown until every holding has a verified price or one you supplied.

The Excel file can't be saved

Close the workbook in Excel and choose a folder you can write to.

For developers

scripts/portfolio_rebalancer/core.py holds the rebalancing maths and its three invariants: buys never exceed the budget in New Money; buys never exceed sale proceeds in Rebalance; and a warning whenever the plan implies moving money between account types. The web version implements the same algorithm and is checked against this file by the website's parity tests. The unit tests live in the source repository (files/tests) and are not included in the ZIP:

.venv/bin/python -m pip install pytest
.venv/bin/python -m pytest files/tests -q

On Windows use .venv\Scripts\python.exe.
