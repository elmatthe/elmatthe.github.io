How it works

  • 1 · Enter holdings

    Ticker, shares, local price, row currency, target weight and (optionally) account type for up to 50 positions.

  • 2 · Choose a mode

    New Money: buy-only, capped at your budget. Rebalance: no new cash; sells fund buys.

  • 3 · Review the plan

    Buy/Sell/Hold actions, trade values in reporting and local currency, trade shares, post-trade shares, and warnings.

Three invariants the plan always respects

  • Budget cap: in New Money mode, total buys never exceed the budget; buys are scaled down proportionally if needed.
  • Sells fund buys: in Rebalance mode, total buys never exceed sell proceeds.
  • Cross-account funding: with account types shown, you're warned when buys in one account (e.g. TFSA) aren't covered by sells in that same account, or when a ticker trades in several accounts.

Data sources & privacy

  • Your holdings never leave your device. Calculations run locally. With live prices on, only ticker symbols and FX pair symbols (for example CADUSD=X) are requested.
  • Yahoo Finance is the only price and FX source. Because Yahoo blocks direct requests from other websites, the web app relays requests through public CORS proxies and checks every response is Yahoo data for the requested symbol. Quotes are cached for five minutes in this tab.
  • Fallback FX rates (used when live data is off or unavailable) are fixed approximations and are labelled as such in the results.

Troubleshooting

Live prices are unavailable

Yahoo Finance is reached through free public relays whose availability varies. When live data can't be retrieved, each row keeps the price you entered, fixed fallback FX rates are used and labelled, and the plan and exports still work. Retry after a minute, or untick live prices. Optional relay settings saved in the Stock tool's Settings tab also apply here in the same browser; they aren't needed for normal use.

“Not found on Yahoo Finance” for a Canadian or international ticker

Use Yahoo suffixes: .TO (TSX), .V (TSX-V), .L (London), .T (Tokyo), .DE, .PA, .HK, and so on, and set the row currency to match. Prefixes like TSX: are converted automatically.

Target weights don't add up to 100%

That's allowed: weights are normalized, so 10/10/20 is treated as 25/25/50. The weight total turns amber when it isn't exactly 100% as a reminder.

The desktop launcher stops with an error

The launcher names the component that failed and keeps a log in files/test-logs/setup-and-run-latest.log. See the setup guide.
