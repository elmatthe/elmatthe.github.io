How it works

  • 1 · Enter holdings

    Ticker exactly as your broker shows it, shares (fractions allowed), target weight and, optionally, the account type. Up to 50 holdings.

  • 2 · Prices are found for you

    When you finish typing a ticker, the tool identifies the listing (exchange, currency, pence or pounds) and fetches its latest price from the source you chose, with the date and provider shown.

  • 3 · Review the plan

    Buy, Sell or Hold for each holding with shares, amounts and weight before → after. The plan appears only when every holding that matters has a verified price.

Three invariants the plan always respects

  • Budget cap: in New Money mode, total buys never exceed the budget; buys are scaled down proportionally if needed.
  • Sells fund buys: in Rebalance mode, total buys never exceed sell proceeds.
  • Cross-account funding: with account types shown, you're warned when buys in one account (e.g. TFSA) aren't covered by sells in that same account, or when a ticker trades in several accounts.

Web app

Runs entirely in this page. Prices come from one of three kinds of source: the free source (no key; Yahoo Finance data through this site's market-data gateway, when that gateway is available: the Market data step says whether it is), a provider you choose with your own key, or a price you enter or import, which is then labelled as user-supplied everywhere. Exchange rates are official Bank of Canada rates (ECB if the Bank of Canada is unreachable).

      Methodology

      Valuation
      Value = shares × price (pence converted to pounds) × exchange rate to the reporting currency. Rates are "reporting currency per 1 unit", derived through CAD from Bank of Canada fixings.
      Targets
      Target weights are normalized to 100%; the plan says so when they do not add up to 100.
      New Money
      Pool = current value + budget. Each underweight holding needs target × pool − value; if those needs exceed the budget, all buys are scaled by the same factor. Nothing is sold.
      Rebalance
      Pool = current value. Overweight holdings are sold down to target; buys are scaled so they never exceed sell proceeds. If no sell is needed, no buy can be funded and buys are shown as Hold.
      Hold band
      Trades smaller than 0.01% of the pool are shown as Hold.
      Whole shares
      Optional: buys and sells are rounded down to whole shares, buys are trimmed one share at a time if they no longer fit the cash, then leftover cash buys one more share of the most underweight holding while a share fits. Cash left over is reported.
      No plan without prices
      Every holding with shares or a target needs a price from your chosen source in a confirmed currency (or a price you supply), no older than three missed trading sessions. Older-than-latest prices must be accepted first. Holdings with 0 shares and a 0% target are left out.

      Data sources & privacy

      • Your holdings never leave your device. Calculations run in this page and nothing about your portfolio is stored or sent. The page sends only ticker symbols to the price source (the free source's gateway, or the provider you choose) and currency codes to the central banks. Shares, targets and account types are never sent.
      • Free source (no key): Yahoo Finance chart data relayed by this site's own market-data gateway (a small Cloudflare Worker with a fixed list of exchanges). It is switched off until written permission for automated retrieval and public redistribution of that data is on record: Yahoo's endpoint is unofficial and its terms restrict automated collection and redistribution. While it is off or unreachable, the Market data step says so and the other sources work as before. When on, its prices may be delayed (often 15–20 minutes during trading), are shown with their trading date and time and never as real-time, and the gateway's saved copies (up to 5 minutes) are labelled with their age. Like any source, it is not an exchange's official price.
      • Prices: fetched with your own key from Alpha Vantage (US, Toronto, TSX Venture, London, Xetra, Shanghai, Shenzhen; 25 requests per day free), Marketstack (end-of-day closes, many exchanges; 100 requests per month free, each symbol counts as one) or Finnhub (US listings; 60 per minute free). Each holding shows the provider, the price type (for example the end-of-day close), the trading date and the currency. Prices are not called real-time unless the provider says so. See the provider matrix for coverage, limits and terms.
      • Identity before price: the ticker you type is kept exactly. Internally it is matched to one listing (exchange and currency) from a small curated reference with sources, or from the provider's own answer, and a price is accepted only if it is for that listing. For example YNVD.NE (and YNVD.TO, as Questrade shows the same fund) is the NVIDIA (NVDA) Yield Shares Purpose ETF on Cboe Canada, in CAD; both spellings are the same instrument. The free plans of the key-based providers do not cover it, so with those it shows Not covered until you supply a price. The free source, when available, asks for YNVD.NE on Cboe Canada and uses the answer only if it is for that listing in CAD. It is never priced as a Toronto Stock Exchange symbol, from NVIDIA shares or from another ETF.
      • Sample portfolio: fourteen ordinary holdings with targets totalling 100%, including one share each of Toyota (Tokyo, JPY), SAP (Xetra, EUR), Vodafone (London, quoted in pence and converted to pounds) and Kweichow Moutai (Shanghai, CNY) at 2.5% each. They are priced, valued and planned exactly like the others; Remove international examples removes those four only while they are unchanged. The sample contains no prices.
      • Keys: kept in this browser only (this tab, unless you tick Remember), sent only to their own provider, never in exports or messages. "Forget all keys" removes them and everything fetched with them. Request counts shown are what this browser sent; your provider counts every use of the key.
      • Exchange rates: Bank of Canada daily average rates (terms), with the European Central Bank's reference rates as a fallback. Pairs not involving CAD are derived through CAD.
      • No public relays and no shared keys. The only server this page may use for prices is this site's own gateway for the free source, and only while it is switched on.

      Troubleshooting

      A holding says “Not covered”

      None of the available sources covers that exchange (for example Cboe Canada or Tokyo on free key plans, or an exchange outside the free source's list). Add a key for a provider that covers it, or choose Use my own price on that holding. The plan then says it includes a user-supplied price.

      The free source says it is not available

      The Market data step says why: this site's market-data gateway has not been set up, is switched off pending permission for its data, or could not be reached. Nothing is fetched from it in those cases. Use a provider with your own key, or your own prices; Check again asks the gateway once more. If the free source answered but a holding failed (busy, rate-limited, or the answer did not match the listing), that holding says so and keeps no price.

      Alpha Vantage says to slow down, or that the daily limit is reached

      A "slow down" (per-second) notice is temporary: the request is retried automatically. A daily-limit notice stops Alpha Vantage requests from this browser until 00:00 UTC; the provider's own wording is shown. Use Check again (one request) if you think it has reset, or price the remaining holdings with another saved source.

      The plan is withheld

      At least one holding with shares or a target has no usable price (not covered, not found, a currency the provider did not confirm, or a price too old). Each one is listed with its reason. Nothing is filled in for you.

      Target weights don't add up to 100%

      That's allowed: weights are normalized, so 10/10/20 is treated as 25/25/50, and the plan says so.

      The desktop launcher stops with an error

      The launcher names the component that failed and keeps a log in files/test-logs/setup-and-run-latest.log. See the setup guide.
