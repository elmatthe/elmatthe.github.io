How it works

  • 1 · Enter holdings

    Ticker, shares, local price, row currency, target weight and (optionally) account type for up to 50 positions.

  • 2 · Choose a mode

    New Money: buy-only, capped at your budget. Rebalance: no new cash; sells fund buys.

  • 3 · Review the plan

    Buy/Sell/Hold actions, trade values in reporting and local currency, trade shares, post-trade shares, and warnings.

Three invariants the plan always respects

  • Budget cap: in New Money mode, total buys never exceed the budget; buys are scaled down proportionally if needed.
  • Sells fund buys: in Rebalance mode, total buys never exceed sell proceeds.
  • Cross-account funding: with account types shown, you're warned when buys in one account (e.g. TFSA) aren't covered by sells in that same account, or when a ticker trades in several accounts.

Live prices (optional)

Uses your own free Alpha Vantage API key (US and TSX/TSX-V stocks and ETFs, some US mutual funds; 25 requests per day). Free keys are for personal use. The key stays in this browser and is sent only to Alpha Vantage, inside each request's address as Alpha Vantage requires. By default it is kept for this tab only; tick Remember to keep it on this device until you remove it. Scripts running on this website can read either storage, so use a free key that you can replace.

Data sources & privacy

  • Your holdings never leave your device. Calculations run locally and nothing about your portfolio is stored or sent. The page requests only currency codes from the central banks and, if you add a key, ticker symbols from Alpha Vantage.
  • Exchange rates: Bank of Canada daily average rates (terms), with the European Central Bank's reference rates as a fallback. Pairs not involving CAD are derived through CAD and labelled as such. Rates are cached in this browser for an hour. Until they load, built-in estimates dated in the table are used and flagged in the plan.
  • Prices: what you type, or the latest price from Alpha Vantage using your own key. The key is kept in this browser only: for the current tab (session storage) unless you tick Remember, which keeps it in local storage until you press Remove key. It is sent only to Alpha Vantage. Prices fetched with a key are cached under that key alone and deleted when you change or remove it. Browser storage can be read by any script on this website; this page loads no third-party scripts. The price's trading date is shown, and older than four days is flagged. Quotes are cached for 15 minutes to save your 25 daily requests. Market indexes, mutual-fund codes such as Canadian TDB902, and exchanges outside the US, Toronto, London, Frankfurt, Bombay and Shanghai/Shenzhen are not available; enter those prices yourself.
  • No public relays. Earlier versions fetched Yahoo Finance through free public CORS relays. They were unreliable and Yahoo's terms do not permit that use, so they have been removed.

Troubleshooting

Live prices are unavailable

Live prices need your own free Alpha Vantage key, which allows 25 requests per day (about two per new ticker, then one per ticker; results are cached). When a price can't be fetched, the row keeps the price you typed; a row with no price stops the run and is listed so you can fill it in. Your inputs are never cleared.

Exchange rates show “Built-in estimate”

The Bank of Canada and the ECB could not be reached, so dated estimates are shown and the plan warns about them. Press Reload official rates, or type your broker's rates into the table.

A Canadian or international ticker isn't found

Use exchange suffixes: .TO (TSX), .V (TSX-V), .L (London), .DE (Xetra), and set the row currency to match. Prefixes like TSX: are converted automatically. A listing whose currency the provider doesn't confirm keeps your typed price.

Target weights don't add up to 100%

That's allowed: weights are normalized, so 10/10/20 is treated as 25/25/50. The weight total turns amber when it isn't exactly 100% as a reminder.

The desktop launcher stops with an error

The launcher names the component that failed and keeps a log in files/test-logs/setup-and-run-latest.log. See the setup guide.
